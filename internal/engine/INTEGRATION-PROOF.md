## Limits and evidence storage

An arbitrary installed Gashki executable does not acquire source provenance from
its version string. Unproved missing-send recovery remains handover. Operator
advice does not assert checked missing-receipt facts without their evidence.
The engine publishes those facts only inside a passed protected-file guard,
after source, send and server checks. It checks the bindings again on resume.
It clears facts after a valid-state binding conflict. If an immutable authority
is damaged, the store refuses inspection and control writes instead.
Confirmed cursor recovery rechecks the saved send, server, inputs and config
before it waits again. Abrupt death with unanchored controller files stays
conservative. Direct completion lost without a receipt also remains handover.
There is no general exactly-once delivery claim.

These stubs prove arguments and mechanics. They do not prove vendor permissions,
authentication, billing or model behavior. Codex pane protection detects changes
after a turn. It does not prevent writes during that turn. Tier C remains pending.
Publication and the default-entry-point gate remain separate work.

Routine fixtures require a 2 GiB free-space reserve. Evidence collection rejects
fixture data above 512 MiB.
They save compact hashes and remove their exact roots after owned processes
settle. Linux evidence uses disk storage. It does not fill the RAM temporary
filesystem. The checked compressed evidence has SHA-256
`7671d925a4f198893e71d9107a0a67389cb92e93880484f3d0e93a0084ca1891`.
